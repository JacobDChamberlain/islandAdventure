# Meshy.ai prompts

Prompts used to generate art for Island Adventure. Style target: **vibrant
stylized** (bright colors, clean chunky shapes, soft cel-shaded), consistent
across every model so the world looks like one game.

## Universal settings (apply to every generation)
- Mode: **Text to 3D**
- Art style preset: **Stylized / Cartoon** (if offered) — avoid "Realistic".
- **PBR textures: ON**
- **Symmetry: ON** (for characters/props that are symmetrical)
- Topology: **Quad** if available (better for rigging), Target polycount **Low–Medium**.
- Always ask for: *full body, plain/neutral background, single object, facing forward.*

---

## Hero character — "Pip", the island explorer (v1)

> A stylized 3D cartoon adventurer child, hero of a colorful platformer game.
> Big head, small body, exaggerated cute proportions. Windswept teal hair with
> coral-orange tips, aviator goggles resting on the forehead, big friendly eyes.
> Wears a warm-orange explorer vest over a teal shirt with rolled sleeves, brown
> gloves, and chunky boots. A large adventurer's backpack with a glowing gem on
> it, and a short flowing scarf. Vibrant saturated colors, clean smooth shapes,
> soft cel-shaded look. Full body, symmetrical, standing in a relaxed A-pose
> with arms held slightly away from the sides, facing forward, neutral
> expression. Game-ready character, clean topology, PBR textures, plain
> background.

**Why the A-pose:** arms held away from the body makes Meshy's auto-rigging and
animation work far more reliably. Do NOT ask for an action pose for the hero.
